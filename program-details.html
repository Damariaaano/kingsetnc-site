Program Details

A closer look at how the internship is structured and what participants can expect.

This page brings together the key details of the KINGSETNC Summer Internship Program, including structure, learning areas, stipend information, conduct expectations, and completion.

Location

Charlotte, North Carolina

Start Date

June 10

Program Type

Summer Internship Program

Eligibility

Open to male youth

Stipend

Monthly stipend provided based on participation and engagement

Structure

Program Structure

  • Duration: 6-8 Weeks
  • Schedule: 2-3 Days Per Week
  • Session Length: 2-4 Hours Per Session
  • Participants: 1-3 interns

Exposure

Outings and Experience

  • Visits to businesses and professional environments
  • Mentorship opportunities
  • Real-world experiences outside of the classroom

Completion

Successful Completion

  • Certificate of Completion
  • Summary of skills developed

Digital Skills

Creative and Digital Learning

  • Video editing
  • Basic graphic design
  • Computer troubleshooting
  • Introduction to digital tools

Life Skills

Everyday Growth

  • Cooking and meal preparation
  • Communication and confidence
  • Time management
  • Discipline and accountability

Practical Development

Hands-On Practice

  • Problem-solving
  • Task completion
  • Learning and applying new skills

Stipend

Why It Is Provided

  • Encourage commitment and responsibility
  • Reward consistency and effort
  • Reinforce accountability and participation

Requirements

Stipend Requirements

  • Attend scheduled sessions consistently
  • Actively participate in all activities
  • Complete assigned tasks and projects
  • Maintain respectful and appropriate behavior

Conduct

Behavior and Conduct

  • Show respect to mentors, peers, and all environments
  • Follow instructions and program guidelines
  • Maintain a positive and teachable attitude
  • Represent KINGSETNC with integrity at all times

Program Classification

The KINGSETNC Summer Internship Program is a youth development and mentorship program. Participants are not employees of KINGSETNC. Any stipend provided is for participation, engagement, and completion of program activities, not wages for employment.

Removal From Program

  • Disrespectful behavior
  • Repeated failure to follow instructions
  • Legal trouble or unlawful activity

KINGSETNC is a warm, structured space for discipline, mentorship, and real-life growth.
