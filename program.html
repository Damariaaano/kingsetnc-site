Programs

A balanced look at how KINGSETNC supports growth beyond any single offering.

KINGSETNC is shaped as a broader journey of mentorship, discipline, identity, faith, and real-life development. The summer internship is simply one program the foundation is preparing to offer, not the purpose of the nonprofit itself.

Program Direction

The nonprofit leads with mission first, then builds programs around that mission.
