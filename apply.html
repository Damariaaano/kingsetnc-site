KINGSETNC Internship Application

This application is for the KINGSETNC Summer Internship Program. The program is designed to help young men develop discipline, responsibility, and real-life skills through mentorship, hands-on learning, and structured activities. Participants will have opportunities to learn skills such as cooking, video editing, problem-solving, and personal development while being part of a positive and growth-focused environment.
